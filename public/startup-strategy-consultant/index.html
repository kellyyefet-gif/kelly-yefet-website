COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we work together.

What does a startup strategy consultant do?

A startup strategy consultant helps founders turn an opportunity into a set of decisions: who the customer is, what problem matters, how the business creates value, what assumptions need validation and what to prioritize next.

How is this different from a business coach?

This is project-based operating work, not general coaching. The engagement is tied to a specific business decision or opportunity and ends with a documented strategy and prioritized path forward.
