COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we work together.

What does a go-to-market strategy consultant do?

A go-to-market consultant helps decide who to target, how to position the offer, which channels to prioritize, what needs to be validated before launch and how to sequence the work into an executable plan.

Do you only create the strategy?

No. My work is designed to stay close to execution. Depending on scope, I can help run validation, shape the launch, coordinate specialists and use early results to adjust the plan.
