COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we work together.

What does fractional strategy and growth leadership mean?

It means bringing an experienced operator into the leadership team on a part-time basis to own a defined strategic or growth mandate. The role combines decision-making, prioritization and hands-on leadership of the work.

How is this different from hiring an agency?

An agency usually owns a defined channel or set of deliverables. I work on the company side: deciding what should be prioritized, aligning the work across functions and helping determine which internal or external resources should execute it.
